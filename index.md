# 🔐 linux-hello - Secure Facial Login Made Easy

## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to **linux-hello**! This application provides secure facial authentication for Linux, similar to Windows Hello. It uses advanced methods to recognize your face and works well even if your face is turned slightly. 

## 🛠️ Features

- **Facial Recognition:** Quickly and securely log in with your face.
- **TPM Support:** Benefit from added security features provided by your Trusted Platform Module.
- **User-Friendly GUI:** Easily manage settings through an intuitive graphical interface.
- **Rotation-Invariant Detection:** Recognizes your face from different angles.

## 🌟 System Requirements

- **Operating System:** Linux (various distributions supported)
- **Memory:** At least 2 GB of RAM
- **Storage:** Minimum 100 MB of free disk space
- **Camera:** A compatible webcam for facial detection

## 💡 Usage Instructions

1. **Open the Application:** After installing, find the application in your applications menu and launch it.
2. **Setup Your Profile:** Follow the prompts to register your face. Ensure good lighting for best results.
3. **Log In Securely:** Use facial recognition to log into your system.

## 📄 Troubleshooting
